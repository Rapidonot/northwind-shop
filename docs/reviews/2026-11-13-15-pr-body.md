## A2A-05 prep (row #15): frozen partner script and named answer fixtures

**Decision 0002 §1.** This PR fixes the partner test client's script now, during the credit window. It also records

A2A-05 is built in Window B and will run discovery, auth, scopes, out-of-scope rejection and the scoped order for

### What is in it
- **Script:** `evals/fixtures/a2a-05-partner/script.json` holds 9 turns for one US shopper:
  - 7 product questions on real catalog products;
  - `oos-01-refund`, the out-of-scope request;
  - `order-01-breakwater`, the scoped order.

  `script.sha256` holds the digest of the file's exact bytes, and every fixture records it.
- **Fixtures:** `answers/<name>.json`, one per turn, recorded through `stream_answer`. Never `run_turn`, so #13's
  output check applies. Each holds:
  - the saved contexts;
  - shown and sent text;
  - citations;
  - its `assistant_id`;
  - its upstream calls;
  - disclosure flags.
- **Tests:** `evals/tests/test_a2a05_partner.py` and `test_a2a05_partner_criteria.py`, tagged `A2A-05`.
  - `test_partner_turn` (`e2e`, `llm_suite("a2a-05-partner")`) records in a window. In replay it re-runs
    `stream_answer` on the saved contexts, with no database, and must reproduce each fixture exactly.
  - It and check 2, the output-check payload, run only while a fixture's `assistant_id` is current. Otherwise
    they skip with "stale: assistant changed since the provisional recording; RECORD-ALL (#18) re-records".
  - Checks 1 and 3–9 always run in `make test`:
    - identity and digests;
    - citations come from this turn's sources;
    - nothing internal and no canary;
    - the secret/PAN scanner;
    - no price, stock or delivery values, including in chunk text;
    - no refund or order claimed done;
    - per-sentence AI disclosure;
    - exact cassette binding, with no failed tool result.
  - `served_fixture` is the fail-closed serving entry point that A2A-05 moves into its package. It refuses:
    - `never_served`, which it reads from the script;
    - a stale fixture;
    - a fixture that does not match the script;
    - the order narrative, unless it comes with an order artifact and order tools.
- **Recording safety:** before and after each turn, a read-only check confirms the index is main's: `data_version`
  2, `index_version` `389cae01…`, and `dataset_sha256` equal to `data/fixtures/manifest.json`. Recording also
  requires match-by-hash, and any unexpected upstream request fails the turn before a fixture is written.
