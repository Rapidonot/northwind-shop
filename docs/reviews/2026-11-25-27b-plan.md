## Stage A
- **`northwind_common/returns_policy.py`:** a pure evaluator plus a facts table. Each fact holds its document ID and the
  exact sentence. Windows US 30, CA 45, UK 28, EU 60 days from the delivered scan; UK and EU 14-day statutory period;
  inserts: US not once unpackaged, **CA unused only, UK and EU sealed only** (the draft named US and UK); wear and tear to
  the repair program.
  - Conditions (the draft's `unworn` is split, because the insert rule needs packaging): `sealed`, `opened_unused`, `unused`
    (packaging not stated), `worn_or_washed`, `damaged_on_arrival`, `wear_and_tear`, `not_stated`.
  - **Rules, first match wins:**
    1. not delivered: ineligible
    2. more than one matching line: ambiguous
    3. wear and tear: ineligible (repair program); ambiguous inside 14 days in UK and EU (as rule 9)
    4. damaged on arrival: ambiguous (no policy covers it)
    5. after the window: ineligible
    6. clearance: ineligible in US and CA, and in UK and EU after 14 days; otherwise go on
    7. condition not stated: ambiguous (the assistant asks)
    8. worn or washed: ambiguous inside 14 days in UK and EU, else ineligible
    9. insert: `sealed` eligible; CA also `unused` and `opened_unused`; elsewhere `unused` is ambiguous (asks about the
       packaging) and `opened_unused` is ineligible, except inside 14 days in UK and EU: ambiguous, because the seal rule and
       the no-reason right conflict
    10. any other unused item, opened or not: eligible (the policy says nothing about packaging)
  - Rules 2 and 4 come before the window on purpose: policy text settles neither, so a person decides, at any age.
  - Each result has a reason code, the facts used and the next step. The day of delivery is day 0; the window's last day is in.
  - Clearance: no product in data v3 is marked clearance, so it is unit-tested on a synthetic product only (stated limit).
- **`services/mcp-policy`:** PR 1's server shape (`mcp_orders/server.py`, `mcp_gate`, error envelope, card-data and
  unknown-key refusal). Reuses `northwind_mcp_orders.book.OrderBook` for the own-order read: same `not_found` bytes.
  - Resources `northwind-policy://returns/{region}` and `…/shipping/{region}`, the token's region only, from public
    `POL-*` documents after a manifest check. `INT-*` and canaries are never served. **No warranty document exists, so no
    warranty resource** (CC-10's policy resource is covered; warranty stays a stated gap).
  - `evaluate_return_eligibility(order_id, variant_id, condition)`: read-only.
- **`request_return` core (`mcp-orders`; MCP handler in stage B):** `order_id`, `variant_id`, `condition`, `idempotency_key`.
  Re-evaluates on the server. Eligible: `requested`; ambiguous: `needs_review`; ineligible: business error,
  nothing written. Keys per (turn, key) as the cart's `_idempotent` in `mcp_catalog/cart.py`; a second open request for the
  same line returns the first. In-memory, like the cart.
- **Labelled cases:** about 60 in `services/mcp-policy/tests/return_cases.jsonl`, `reviewed_by: model`, written from the
  policy text before the evaluator and sha256-pinned. A disagreement is stop 7, never a silent label edit.
