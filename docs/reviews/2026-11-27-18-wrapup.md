### 2a. EV-029: the cause (2026-11-27, all at $0)

- **The miss was the judge's request, not the answerer's.**
  - The failed result holds EV-029's complete answer.
  - Its detail, "400: Error code: 400 - …", is the Python `anthropic` SDK's `APIStatusError`. `run_item` catches it
    around `judge.judge(...)`, after `answerer.answer(...)` returned.
  - In a rescore the judge goes through the same `eval-answers` proxy.
- **Mechanism:** the judge prompt includes the turn's live tool values.
  - In-process mcp-catalog calls `CatalogLog.add` as each tool call **finishes**.
  - `answerers.live_of` numbers the results by position (`live:get_price:<n>`), and `render_sources` renders them in
    that order.
  - The proxy's `tool_use` reorder (row #16, `tool_order.py`) covers the `tool_result` blocks of the model's own
    requests, not this text. So with parallel calls, the judge request's bytes depend on which call finished first.
- **Proof** (`$TMPDIR/w13script/ev029_reorder.py`, not committed): EV-029 alone, with the in-process `get_price`
  delayed so its 17 parallel calls finish in reverse order.
  - **1 miss, `eval-judge`, the same error as the rescore.**
  - The control (same patch, no delay) has 0 misses. The answerer's requests hit in both.
  - The diff (`2026-11-27-18-ev029-judge-miss.diff.txt`) shows the same 17 blocks, numbered 2–18, in reverse
    variant order.
  - Earlier: 8 lone and 3 EV-001..029 replays had no miss, and forcing the CLI stream watchdog had no effect.
- **Exposure:** judge requests with 2 or more live blocks: 6 in each of `eval-answers`, `-r2`, `-r3` and
  `eval-judge-repeat` (EV-029, -030, -043, -205, -213, -245).
  - Against the answerer's `tool_use` order, `eval-answers` matches on 5 of the 6. EV-030 does not, though that
    reading is rough.
  - `-r2`, `-r3` and `eval-judge-repeat` were recorded out of `tool_use` order on 2–3 items each (EV-029, -030,
    -245).
- **Effect:** any replay of an exposed item can miss when its parallel calls finish in another order. That covers
  `make ci`'s eval gate, the rescore and W13. After about 2026-12-22 a miss can no longer be re-recorded.
- **Rescore:** not rerun, waiting on the user.
