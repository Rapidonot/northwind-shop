## Method

1. **Claims.** Every claim the judge marked unsupported with kind `site` or `policy`, taken from the item's run-1 judge
   cassette in `cassettes/eval-answers/` (the request whose `<answer>` is exactly run 1's answer and whose query is the
   item's). Two counts: the judge's raw kind, and v6's final kind after M1.
2. **Classes.**
   - **check:** the claim restates a `FIXED_SENTENCES` value (`services/assistant/src/northwind_assistant/output_check.py`)
     that the output check put there. A fixed sentence is the check's when the answer sent holds it and the model's raw
     text does not. The raw text is the item's `eval-answerer:<ID>` cassettes, using the recording closest to the
     answer sent (a re-recorded item also has older recordings). Matched by difflib ratio at least 0.5.
   - **self:** the assistant describing what it can or cannot do (subject I, we, the assistant, this assistant).
   - **other:** any other site or policy claim.
3. **Target:** at most 5 model-made claims (self plus other). Above that, it goes to #17. The prompt is not changed here
   (the user, item 4).
