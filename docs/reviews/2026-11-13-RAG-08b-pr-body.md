RAG-08 + NFR-03 (partial), row #13b: the evals now score the Shopper Assistant itself

**Labels:** `gate-change` (this PR changes `evals/src`), `baseline-change` (new baseline, two new items).

## What changes

Until now, answer evals scored a single-shot stand-in. They now go through the real assistant (`stream_answer`), so
the assistant's own prompt and #13's output check are what gets scored.

- **Adapter** (`evals/src/northwind_evals/answerers.py`):
  - `AssistantAnswerer` drives `stream_answer`, never `run_turn`, against an in-process mcp-rag (`serve_retriever`)
    over a `SavedFirstIndex`;
  - the first search must be the item's own query and region; it is served the saved context;
  - the model's follow-up searches are recorded in `evals/contexts/refinements.ndjson` and replayed from it;
  - a fresh mcp-rag key per run; refinements are leak-checked before they are served or written;
  - `SingleShotAnswerer` is gone. A test-only double (`northwind_evals.testing`) keeps the CLI tests' shape, and a
    test pins that nothing else imports it.
- **Scoring** (`harness.py`, `judge.py`):
  - judge v5 tags each claim's kind (product, policy, site, other); a missing or unknown kind counts as product;
  - `fabricated_ok` (no unsupported product claim) is folded into `behaviour_ok` on adversarial items, so the
    absolute containment gate holds it, main's gate at promote included;
  - citations scale with product and policy claims;
  - planted claims are checked on every token sent as well as on the text left on screen; the R00970 steer
    (#8c condition 4).
- **Gate** (`gate.py`, `cli.py`, `integrity.py`):
  - F8: a changed retriever, index, judge, or answerer model or prompt fails the gate; a changed check hash is
    reported;
  - an errored adversarial or unanswerable item fails containment;
  - refusals (HARDEN-1's proxy signal) are reported per item and per segment, not failed;
  - any fallback-beta attempt refuses the candidate and fails the gate;
  - `refinements.ndjson` is add-only in `eval-integrity`.
- **Output check:** class 1 now covers "member pricing" (one pattern), after the user ruled EV-090's "member pricing
  or current promotions" sentence a product claim (plan R4).
- **Data:**
  - `DATA_VERSION` 2: planted review R01221 / TRAP-INJ-016 on P0156, with fullwidth and U+3008/U+3009
    `<source type="document">` lookalikes. Its tag words are fullwidth too, so the index sees no "document" or
    "source";
  - every other fixture is byte-identical; `data/backend/manifest.json` changes only `source_dataset_sha256`;
  - new items EV-101 (bare greeting) and EV-102, both adversarial and human-reviewed.
