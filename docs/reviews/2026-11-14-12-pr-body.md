    region (`tools.record_live`), never from text.

    currencies and magnitudes. An amount passes only when a `get_price` result this turn has the same amount and
    currency. A figure the shopper wrote is blocked too (D5).
  - **Stock (D3):**
    - a count must equal one checked variant's units;
    - "in stock" or "available" needs every checked variant above zero;
    - "out of stock", "sold out" or "unavailable" needs every checked variant at zero;
    - "low" never passes;
    - a governing negation flips the polarity, and any other negation in the clause blocks the claim.
    - **Any "available" or "unavailable" is a stock claim**, whatever precedes it (the user's re-read). The only
      exception is "in" plus a plural range with no time word.
    - Stock phrases accept hyphens ("in-stock", "out-of-stock", "sold-out").

    sentence, which says the same thing.
  - **Member-price wordings** join class 1, measured with no false positive on the recorded answers.
  - **Fixed sentences:**

    - A replacement that repeats the sentence before it is collapsed (EV-090).
    - Text from two model calls in one turn is joined with a paragraph break, so a preamble cannot glue onto the
      answer as a bare domain (W1).
  - A money match that ends a stream chunk on "." or "," is held, so "$263." is not read as 263 before its
