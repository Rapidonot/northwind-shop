    - order or payment confirmation;
    - refund authority and stock claims;

  - **Invented citation markers** are removed from the held text before matching. Citations come from the text left on screen.
- **Holding back:**
  - the trailing word, plus at most L = 64 view characters from a candidate start;
  - every pattern is at most L wide (tested);
  - every window is measured in matching-view characters: NFKD, then marks, format and default-ignorable characters dropped, then NFKC, confusables and casefold;
  - each delta costs time in proportion to its own length;
  - text is released in the model's own deltas, so nothing changes shape when nothing is blocked;
  - if part of a blocked sentence was already sent, the check sends `reset` and re-sends only checked text.
