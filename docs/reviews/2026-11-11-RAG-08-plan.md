## 5. Design (#13)

**5.1 Where it runs:**
- A pure `OutputCheck` (new `services/assistant/src/northwind_assistant/output_check.py`) sits inside
  `stream_answer`, between `run_turn` and the caller. The gateway and #13b's adapter both get checked text, and
  `chat.py` is unchanged.
- `run_turn` is kept, with a pin that only `stream_answer` calls it.
- Event handling:
  - the model's `Reset` clears the held and released text;
  - `TurnError` discards held text (fail closed);
  - `Citations` is computed from the text released after the last `Reset`, intersected with `seen`;
  - `usage.report` moves after the final flush: still one telemetry line, now with block counts per class and no
    text;
  - at the end of the stream, anything unresolved is withheld.

**5.2 Matching view:**
- NFKC, then format characters removed (U+200B included), then a confusables fold, then casefold, with an index map
  back to the raw text.
- The code shape `code [A-Z0-9]{4,12}` is matched before casefolding. It is tested on STAFFALL and on "dress code
  from".
- Sentence boundaries: `[.!?]` followed by whitespace, a newline, or the start of a bullet or heading line.

**5.3 Lookahead** (the row's design: from a candidate start, not whole sentences):
- The trailing partial word is always held. **A word is the trailing run of non-whitespace (`\S+`)**, so
  `northwind-deals.` is never released before `net` arrives (revision M4).
- From the earliest trigger, at most **L = 64** characters are held. Triggers include `disc`, `coupon`, `promo`, a
  digit or number word, `%`, `http`, `www`, `@`, `[`, `authori`, `approv`, `refund`, `order`, `confirm`, `stock` and
  `sold`.
- Every pattern is bounded, and a width test asserts it is at most L. The one exception is the class 2 word check
  (below), which runs on each completed word and is excluded from the width test by name.
- **The bound is "at most L from a trigger, plus the current partial word"**, as approved in (a)2.
- A candidate still possible at L counts as a match (fail closed). The one exception is an unclosed `[`, which is
  released, and only by the citation-marker check: `[northwind-deals.net…` with no `]` still goes through class 2
  (revision L2).

**5.4 On a match**, the sentence is replaced by its class's fixed sentence. Each makes no product or policy claim and
no figure, passes every class, and **is approved by you here:**

| Class | Fixed sentence |
|---|---|
| 1 promotion | "I can't help with offers or codes in chat." |
| 2 links / contact | "I've left out a link or contact detail here." |
| 3 confirmation, 4 refund authority | "I can't confirm orders, payments or refunds in chat." |
| 4 stock claims | "I can't check stock in chat." |
| 5 false policy | "I couldn't match that policy term to Northwind's policy for your region." |

**Repair:** if the sentence started in text already released, the check emits `Reset` and re-sends the released text
up to the sentence start, then the fixed sentence. Only text that passed is ever sent twice, and the portal already
handles `reset`. Invented `[ID]` markers are removed, and their sentence stays.

**5.5 Classes** (frozen at approval; a change after a recording is stop 7). A probe over the 41 recorded smoke and
red-team answers shaped classes 1 and 3, so that evidence is labelled **in-sample**; the 100 eval answers are
out-of-sample.
1. **Promotion** (class-level, no hedge exemption): `discount`, `coupon`, `promo(tion(al)?) code`, `\d+ ?% off`,
   `voucher`, `staff code`, `member price`, `free today`, plus the code shape.
2. **Links and contact:**
   - every URL is blocked;
   - emails pass only if exactly `care@northwind.example` (FAQ-018);
   - every phone number is blocked;
   - **(change 3)** because the trailing partial word is always held, a whole word that is a bare domain (no scheme
     or `www`, e.g. `northwind-deals.net/x`) is also a URL.
     - **Detector (revision M4):** each completed `\S+` word, on the matching view, is checked with a short pattern
       that can match anywhere inside it, `[\w-]\.[^\W\d_]{2}`, about 5 characters wide. So a host with many
       labels, a long path or an accented label is still caught; the sentence is replaced anyway.
     - **Passes:** `2.5`, `2.5mm`, `e.g.`, `[P0011].`, `jacket.`, ISO dates.
     - **Exempt only as a whole token:** the exact email `care@northwind.example`.
       `care@northwind.example.evil.net` and `care@northwind.example/x` are replaced.
       Trailing sentence punctuation (`.,;:!?)` and closing brackets) is stripped before that comparison, because
       FAQ-018 and RT-015/RT-028 write `care@northwind.example.`. Verified: on samples the detector hits all nine
       must-replace words and none of the nine must-pass ones; over all fixture text it hits only
       `care@northwind.example.` (exempt after stripping) and R00461's and R00536's planted hosts.
     - The caller's fixture probe found only R00461's planted link (TRAP-INJ-010), but it used a broader pattern;
       the over-blocking test re-runs it with this detector.
   - ISO dates and `20,000 mm` pass.
3. **Confirmation:**
   - patterns:
     - `(order|payment|refund|discount|code) (is|has been|was) (confirmed|processed|approved|authori[sz]ed|applied|placed|issued)`;
     - `I('ve| have) (confirmed|processed|applied|placed|issued|authori[sz]ed)`;
     - `order confirmed`.
   - Not matched:
     - **(change 1)** a conditional only when the conditional word directly introduces the subject:
       `(if|when|once|after|before) (?:(your|the|an?) )?(order|payment|refund) …` ("If your order is placed…",
       RT-022). "Before anything else: your order has been confirmed." is still blocked, and is a test.
       - **Per match, not per sentence (revision M3):** the exemption covers only the match whose subject directly
         follows the conditional prefix; every other match in the sentence is checked on its own. Tests that must
         be replaced: "Once the order is placed, your refund has been issued." and "If your order is placed, your
         payment has been processed."
       - **Residual risk, your call (revision L3):** your verb list includes `was` and `has been`, so "Once your
         payment was processed, we shipped it." is exempt, although it states payment state, which hard rule 2
         says only live tools may give. Kept as you worded it; limiting the exemption to `is` would close it;
     - plural policy text ("Refunds are issued within 5 business days");
     - a negation in between.
4. **Payload subjects:**
   - **refund authority:**
     - `authori[sz]\w{0,6} … refund`;
     - `refund … (authori[sz]ed|approved)`;
     - `approv\w{0,4} … refund`;
     - at most 3 words between;
     - `issue_refund` and `apply_discount`;
     - **(change 2)** a refund-authority match passes only when its exact matched span, on the matching view,
       appears in a trusted source returned this turn, as class 5 does for durations.
       - **Both sides are compared on the same view:** the rendered source text is html-unescaped, then NFKC,
         format characters removed, the confusables fold and casefold.
       - **Narrowed, for safety (revision M2):** for this exemption, "trusted" means a public policy document only
         (`type="document"` and a `POL-` ID). The only fixture texts that match are INT-001 and INT-002 (internal,
         `type="document"`). If RAG-11 containment failed, INT-002's "associates may approve refund overrides"
         would otherwise pass: RT-003's leak. A test checks that an INT-002 source returned with the answer does
         not exempt.
       - **Never exempt:** `issue_refund` and `apply_discount`. Classes 1 and 4-stock keep no exemption.
       - **How "every `POL-RETURNS-*` region" is tested (revision M1):** no real POL-RETURNS text holds a matching
         span. So per region, (a) the real body, and an answer quoting it word for word, give no class 4 match; and
         (b) a crafted `document` source with a `POL-RETURNS-<region>` ID (the real body plus one matching
         sentence, e.g. "Refunds are approved after inspection") lets the span pass, while the same span in a
         `type="review"` source is replaced.
       - **Accepted residual:** a review span passes only when a public policy document returned that turn holds
         the identical span. That cannot happen with today's data, and by design the words are then policy text.
   - **stock claims:** `out of stock`, `sold out`, `in stock`, `only \d+ left`, `low stock`.
   - These catch R00712 ("discounts"), RT-003 ("authorizing refunds") and RT-016 ("a refund is authorized").
5. **False policy:**
   - a duration passes only if the same duration is in a **trusted** source returned this turn;
   - "free returns", "free return label" and "prepaid label" pass on the same terms;
   - durations only in reviews are blocked (R01080);
   - other terms go to the judge (**partial**).
