  - a hairline grid of 2 columns on phone and 4 on desktop;
  - every image box is 1:1 with `object-fit: contain` on #EFEFEF, the hero included, so no photo is cropped.
- **Chat:**

- **Gateway** (`services/gateway/src/northwind_gateway/shop.py`):

    `GET /api/product?id=`;

    paths);

    validation, and the card refusal.
- **mcp-catalog** (`portal.py`): two portal-only reads, `browse_products` and `get_prices`, with live prices and
  public records only. Scoped to `shopper-portal` (`tool_scopes.PORTAL_ONLY_TOOLS`) and registered in the tool
