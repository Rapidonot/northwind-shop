## What was built
- **Portal** (`apps/portal/src/`):
  - hash routes (`router.ts`), with no new dependency;
  - every page lists all 10 departments in the top nav, with a 2px ink underline on the selected one, plus the banner
    "Demo store. AI assistant. No real orders, payments or deliveries.", the wordmark, search and the bag count;
  - pages (`pages.tsx`): home (a hero and a grid), department (one heading and "N items", no chip row), search
    results, product (brand line, name without the brand, live price per variant, colour and size, description,
    specifications) and a read-only bag;
  - checkout and orders are placeholders until PR 2, with no card field of any kind.
- **The look** (`theme.css`):
  - the brief's palette, with the accent only on the bag count (ADD TO BAG arrives in PR 2);
  - Archivo 800 for the wordmark, 500–700 for headings, Inter for the body;
  - uppercase only on the wordmark and short buttons;
  - corners 0–4px, no shadow or gradient, 1px rules, and 8px grey separators between product-page sections;
  - a hairline grid of 2 columns on phone and 4 on desktop;
  - every image box is 1:1 with `object-fit: contain` on #EFEFEF, the hero included, so no photo is cropped.
- **Chat:**
  - the existing `ChatPanel` is a side panel on desktop. On a phone it is an ASK NORTHWIND button that opens it
    full screen; full screen is an inference, not in the brief;
  - `Chat.tsx` and `chatStream.ts` are unchanged. `ChatPanel` gained an `onCart` callback, which drives the bag count.
- **Gateway** (`services/gateway/src/northwind_gateway/shop.py`):
  - `GET /api/departments`, `GET /api/products?department=&q=` (photos first, then catalog order) and
    `GET /api/product?id=`;
  - it reads mcp-catalog as `shopper-portal`, with the region from the signed session (`SESSION_PATHS` gains the three
    paths);
  - fixed envelopes: 503 unavailable, 404 `not_found` (an internal product is the same as an unknown one), 422
    validation, and the card refusal.
- **mcp-catalog** (`portal.py`): two portal-only reads, `browse_products` and `get_prices`, with live prices and
  public records only. Scoped to `shopper-portal` (`tool_scopes.PORTAL_ONLY_TOOLS`) and registered in the tool
  register.
- **Static files:**
  - 200 WebP photos, `apps/portal/public/products/{800,1600}/P####.webp`, copied in by the user. Each carries a C2PA
    content-credentials chunk (Anthropic's signed provenance, with no personal data);
  - fonts in `apps/portal/public/fonts/`: `@fontsource-variable/archivo@5.3.0` and `@fontsource-variable/inter@5.3.0`,
    each with its OFL licence beside it.
