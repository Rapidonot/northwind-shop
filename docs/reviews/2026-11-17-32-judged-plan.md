**M1, the tie-break.**
- **Rule:** an unsupported claim whose kind is `policy`, `site` or `other` counts as `product` when its text names a
  catalog product or carries a product or variant ID. That covers:
  - the full product name, case-insensitive;
  - the name without its brand, only if that leaves at least two words, for example "Sorrel Tablecloth";
  - `\bP\d{4}(?:-[A-Z0-9]+)*\b`.
- **Where it applies:** in `parse_verdict`, given the catalog's names. It counts for `fabricated_ok` and `citable`.
- **Fail closed:** names that cannot be loaded raise `JudgeError`; they are never skipped.
