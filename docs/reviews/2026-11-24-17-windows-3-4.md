## The promote (user only, after window 4)

First commit the window-4 outputs on the branch (I do that). Then, from your terminal:

    make -C ~/.northwind/runner eval-promote CHECKOUT=$HOME/Projects/northwind-shop CHECK=1

That is a dry run: it prints the candidate's sha256. Then:

    make -C ~/.northwind/runner eval-promote CHECKOUT=$HOME/Projects/northwind-shop APPROVED_BY=Deeptanshu

- **If a metric regresses** or an item is relabelled (decision 0009), add `ACCEPT_REGRESSION=1
  CANDIDATE_SHA=<hex from CHECK=1>`.
- **After the promote:** I commit the baseline. Then `advisor-reviewer` runs on `make review-diff`, its fixes go in,
  then the single `make ci`, then the gate report.
