### 3.5 Output check (`output_check.py`; amends decision 0015 per its §7)
- **Live values:**
  - `ToolState` gains `prices` (amount, currency) and `stock` (variant, on_hand).
  - They are filled in `_note_tool_results` from successful `get_price` / `check_stock` results: `tool_use` ids
    matched by tool name, the wrapper checked, JSON parsed strictly, and `region` equal to the caller's.
    Anything else is ignored (fail closed).
  - `OutputCheck` reads them live, as it reads `seen`.
  - The model cannot forge these: they come only from the CLI's `ToolResultBlock` for our server's tool.
- **New class `money`:**
  - currency sign plus amount (`$`, `£`, `€`, `A$`/`C$`);
  - amount plus code or word (USD, GBP, EUR, AUD, CAD, dollars, pounds, euros, quid, bucks);
  - number words with a currency word ("a hundred dollars", "twenty quid"), read whole by the existing number
    parser;
  - an amount the parser does not consume exactly is unvouched (fail closed, as for durations).
  - **Exemption `live_price`:** the parsed amount and currency equal a `get_price` result this turn.
  - **D5 (the user, option 1, after §5.1 found EV-029 and EV-030):** a figure the shopper wrote ("under $100") is
    blocked too (fail closed). The prompt gains: "If the shopper gives a price limit, give each live price instead
    of restating the limit." At W3 I report how many price-filter answers got the money fixed sentence, and how
    many hit the 6-turn cap.
  - **"available" (the user's `_asks()` rules):** a hedge passes only when "whether", "if" or a question "which"
    governs the clause holding "available". There may be no comma, semicolon, colon or dash between them, and no
    "but", "and" or "so" followed by a subject word. "which" never counts after a comma or directly before a verb.
    **`_asks()` is a named focus for `security-reviewer`.**
  - **Low, logged (the user's change 6):** a price passes if it equals *any* `get_price` result this turn, not
    necessarily the variant named in the sentence.
  - The check counts every match it let through on a `live_price` or `live_stock` exemption as `live_passed`.
    This is the deterministic count of claims backed only by a live value (§3.6).
- **Stock-count patterns (class `stock`):**
  - number (digit or word) plus `left`, `remaining`, `in stock`, `available`, `units`, `on hand`
    ("three left", "12 in stock");
  - "available" beside a variant (L9): `is/are (currently|still)? available` unless followed by
    `in (sizes?|colou?rs?|fits?)`, plus `available (now|to order|today)` and `(currently|still) available`.
- **Exemption `live_stock` (D3):**
  - a count passes when it equals an `on_hand` this turn;
  - a polarity phrase (in stock, out of stock, sold out, available, low stock) passes only when *every*
    `check_stock` result this turn agrees with it. Two checked variants that differ get the fixed sentence:
    over-blocking, measured and reported;
  - "low stock" / "only N left" with no count match stays blocked.
- **#13's stock patterns are kept as they are.** The exemption only applies when a `check_stock` result exists,
  and #13's tests have none.
- **Member-price wordings:** candidates are `members?[-\s]only (rate|price|deal|offer)s?`,
  `member (rate|deal|offer|savings)s?`, `loyalty (price|rate|discount|reward)s?`. ("Member discount" is already
  class 1 through `discount`.) Each candidate is kept only if its false positives on the recorded answers (§5) are
  0, or you accept them.
- **D4, fixed sentences:**
  - `money`: "I can only give a price from the live price check."
  - `stock` changes from "I can't check stock in chat." (untrue once `check_stock` is attached; 0015 named #16 as
    owner) to "I can only give stock from the live stock check."
  - No test pins the literals (checked: tests read `FIXED_SENTENCES`).
  - The existing test that every fixed sentence passes every class covers both.
- **Collapse repeated fixed sentences (EV-090):** a replacement equal (after `normal()`) to the sentence just
  before it on screen is dropped, whether that sentence was the model's own copy or an earlier replacement. Counts
  still record the block.
- **`hits()` / `payload_hits`** gain the live values. Otherwise every allowed price would count as a bypass in the
  evals.
- The engine, the hold window `L` and the existing patterns are unchanged. A test holds that every new pattern is at
  most `L` wide. `TRIGGER` gains the currency signs and words.

  - A new `evals/tests/test_rag09_live_sources.py`:
