## What this does

- **Source links and product cards (SA-02).**
  - The `citations` SSE event gains `sources`: one card per **cited** source (`source_cards.py`).
    - Trusted sources (product, variant, document) give a title, their first line. A product also gives brand and
      department.
    - A review gives only its ID and type, never its text, because review text is attacker-writable (hard rule 6).
    - There is no price or stock field (hard rule 2).
  - `Citations.sources` is not part of equality, so every existing `Citations(...)` assertion still holds.
  - In the portal, every cited `[ID]` marker links to `#source-<ID>`, its entry in the Sources list. Cited products
    and variants show as inline cards.

    - The answer text is unchanged: a group marker keeps its own commas and spaces.
